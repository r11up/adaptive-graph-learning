FINDING 17 — PLAN C (DATA RE-UPLOADING): NO EFFECT, ON TWO COHORTS
===================================================================
Status: measured, all four cohorts, full, 62 folds / 4,456 subjects
Date:   2026-08-28
Code:   scripts/run_reupload.py
Raw:    results/superseded-prefix/planC/{abide,adhd,mdd,ucla}/reupload_results.json


THE IDEA
--------
FINDING 16 showed the QCNN underfitting: training accuracy never exceeded 0.65
at any register width, and widening the register cost 163x the runtime for
+0.017 accuracy. That points at capacity rather than width.

Data re-uploading (Perez-Salinas et al., Quantum 4:226, 2020) re-injects the
features between convolution stages, each with its own trainable gain, raising
the order of Fourier terms the circuit can express in the data. The register
stays at 8 qubits. Depths 1 and 2 were compared against a plain QCNN and the
three established matched classical heads.

Training accuracy was recorded alongside test accuracy, because the claim being
tested is specifically about capacity, and capacity that does not generalise is
invisible in test accuracy alone.

THE SIX "SIGNIFICANT" RESULTS ARE ONE WEAK COMPARATOR
------------------------------------------------------
Across four cohorts, 60 pairwise tests give 6 at p < 0.05 uncorrected against
an expectation of 3.0 by chance. None survives Bonferroni (0.05/60 = 0.00083;
smallest p anywhere = 0.008).

All six are on the two smallest cohorts, and every one has C-CNN losing:

  UCLA-CNP    Q-Plain  > C-CNN   +0.087  8/10  p = 0.0234
              Q-Reup1  > C-CNN   +0.091  8/10  p = 0.0078
              Q-Reup2  > C-CNN   +0.087  8/10  p = 0.0215
              C-MLP    > C-CNN   +0.087  9/10  p = 0.0137
              C-Linear > C-CNN   +0.111  9/10  p = 0.0137
  ADHD-200    C-Linear > C-CNN   +0.062  7/7   p = 0.0312

Reported alone, "all three quantum models beat the classical CNN on UCLA-CNP
at p < 0.025" is a quantum-advantage headline. The matched classical arms kill
it: C-MLP and C-Linear beat C-CNN by the same margin at the same p-values.

The cause is that the 1-D CNN does not train at these sample sizes. Its
accuracy by cohort: 0.599 (ABIDE, n=1035), 0.532 (MDD, n=2428), 0.500 (UCLA,
n=226), 0.482 (ADHD, n=767) — at or below chance on both small cohorts, with
the largest overfit gap of any model there (+0.246 UCLA, +0.195 ADHD).

THIS IS THE STUDY'S CLEANEST DEMONSTRATION of why unmatched comparators
manufacture quantum advantage, and why every comparison here carries more than
one classical arm. It should be reported as such.

Positive on all four, significant on three; the marginal case is the smallest
fold count. The mechanism is the most robust thing Plan C produced.


THE NULL IS COMPLETE AND IT REPLICATES
--------------------------------------
On the two best-powered cohorts, ALL 15 pairwise comparisons among the six
models are non-significant even uncorrected. Smallest p is 0.136 on ABIDE and
0.339 on MDD. Three quantum and three classical models spanning 18 to 354
parameters are statistically indistinguishable on 45 folds and 3,463 subjects.
The two smaller cohorts add six nominal hits, all attributable to C-CNN
(above), and none surviving correction.

Re-uploading specifically does nothing. Depth 1 gains +0.016 on ABIDE at
p = 0.67 (a coin flip at 11/20 wins) and exactly 0.000 on MDD. Depth 2 is
negative on both.


THE MECHANISM REPLICATES, AND STRENGTHENS
-----------------------------------------
Recording training accuracy turns the null into a mechanism:

                                    ABIDE-I          REST-meta-MDD
  train accuracy vs overfit gap   r = +0.949 **     r = +0.991 ***
                                  (p = 0.0038)      (p = 0.0001)
  train accuracy vs test accuracy r = -0.482        r = -0.765
                                  (p = 0.33)        (p = 0.077)

On MDD the six models rank IDENTICALLY by training accuracy and by overfit gap
-- a perfect ordering. Whatever a model manages to fit beyond the shared signal
becomes variance, and this holds regardless of whether the mechanism supplying
the capacity is quantum or classical.

Re-uploading did what its theory claims: training accuracy rose 0.643 -> 0.652
on ABIDE and 0.588 -> 0.594 on MDD. The added Fourier capacity is real and
usable. It simply bought nothing on held-out data.


TWO THINGS THAT DO NOT REPLICATE — DO NOT REPORT THESE AS GENERAL
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PARAMETER COUNT IS A WEAKER PREDICTOR THAN ACHIEVED TRAINING FIT.
   Parameters vs overfit gap is rho = +0.943 (p = 0.005) on ABIDE but only
   rho = +0.657 (p = 0.156) on MDD. The robust predictor across both cohorts
   is training accuracy actually achieved, not capacity nominally available.
   This is the more sensible quantity anyway: an unused parameter costs
   nothing, and C-MLP's 354 parameters overfit while C-CNN's 242 do not.

2. "AN 18-PARAMETER LINEAR LAYER IS BEST" IS ABIDE-SPECIFIC.
   C-Linear wins on ABIDE (0.616 accuracy, 0.679 AUC, smallest gap). On MDD
   the nominal best is Q-Plain at 0.535, with all six models inside a 0.008
   band and 0/15 tests significant. The honest statement for MDD is that
   everything ties; the linear-layer result should be reported as an ABIDE
   observation, not a general claim.


COST
----
Q-Plain 26.2 s/fold on MDD against 0.2 s/fold for C-Linear -- 131x -- for
0.002 lower accuracy. On ABIDE, 11.1 s against 0.1 s.


CUMULATIVE POSITION
-------------------
Six architectural families now: fidelity kernels, population graphs, the Qiskit
model suite, learned projections (FINDING 14), block ensembles (FINDING 15),
and data re-uploading. In every one, each intervention that helps the quantum
arm helps its matched classical twin at least as much.

Plan C differs from Plans A and B in one respect worth noting: A and B produced
significant results AGAINST the quantum arm, while C produces no signal in
either direction. That is consistent with the capacity account -- A and B
changed the representation, whereas C only added expressive capacity to a model
that was not short of it in a way that mattered.


A NOTE ON SUBSETS
-----------------
Unlike Plans A and B, no misleading subset result preceded this one. The UCLA
smoke test (226 subjects, 10 folds) was run for correctness only and its
numbers were never treated as a result, following the protocol adopted after
FINDINGS 14 and 15 were each misjudged on six-fold subsets.
