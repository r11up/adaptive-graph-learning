FINDING 04 — COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE AND HOW TO ACTUALLY BEAT IT
===============================================================
Status: literature survey, 2024-2026
Date:   2026-08-15
Purpose: identify what published quantum work reports, on what data, under
         what protocol, and where a defensible claim is still available


THE HEADLINE: THE GAP IS THE PROTOCOL, NOT THE ACCURACY
--------------------------------------------------------
Targeted searching found no published quantum machine-learning result on ABIDE
functional connectivity evaluated under Leave-Site-Out cross-validation. Quantum
neuroimaging work either uses different modalities (sMRI slices, EEG, calcium
imaging), different cohorts (UCLA CNP, UK Biobank), or easier validation splits.

That is the opening. Not a higher number — a number that means something.

READING THE NUMBERS HONESTLY
----------------------------
The 96.7% and AUC 0.957 figures are not competitors to this work, and treating
them as targets would be a mistake:

- The 96.7% QSVM result is on autism *screening* data (questionnaire/tabular),
  not fMRI functional connectivity. Different problem, vastly easier.
- HybridVQC's 75.0% is ABIDE, but *structural* MRI slices with a ResNet-18
  feature extractor, under a validation/test split rather than site-held-out.
  It is also explicitly "quantum-inspired", not a quantum kernel or circuit.
- The UCLA CNP quantum kernel result (AUC 0.957) is a genuine quantum kernel,
  but on a single-site cohort where site confounds cannot arise.

Meanwhile the two protocol-matched anchors agree closely with each other and
with this work: Abraham et al. reach ~67% under leave-site-out, and this
implementation's SVM reaches 67.1%. Classical methods under easier CV reach
about 72%. That is the true operating range for ABIDE functional connectivity.

Any ABIDE fMRI result materially above ~72% under genuine leave-site-out should
be treated as a red flag until the split is verified. This is worth stating in
the paper: it protects the work and it is a real service to the subfield.


WHY CHASING 96% WOULD BE THE WRONG MOVE
---------------------------------------
To "match" the headline numbers this work would have to abandon leave-site-out
for a random split. That would inflate the metric by roughly 5-10 points through
site leakage alone, and would make the result non-comparable to the very papers
being cited as motivation. It would also undercut the paper's own stated
rationale for LSO. The number would go up; the contribution would go down.


THE DEFENSIBLE CLAIM AVAILABLE
------------------------------
Not "quantum achieves 96%". Rather:

  "Under site-held-out validation on ABIDE, a quantum kernel outperforms a
   matched classical kernel on identical features and identical folds, with
   paired significance across 20 sites."

An effect as small as +0.03 AUC with p < 0.05 paired over 20 sites is a
stronger scientific claim than 96% on a random split, because it is the first
protocol-clean quantum-vs-classical comparison in this setting. It is also
falsifiable, which the headline numbers largely are not.

Current status of that claim (FINDING 03): quantum kernel AUC 0.592 vs
classical 0.633-0.635, p = 0.945. Not yet achieved, with the search space
essentially unexplored.


CONCRETE LEVERS, RANKED BY EXPECTED EFFECT
------------------------------------------
1. ENCODING BANDWIDTH. The scaling applied to features before angle encoding
   controls the concentration/expressivity trade-off and is the single most
   influential hyperparameter in quantum kernels [4]. It is currently fixed at
   1.0 and has never been tuned. Bandwidth tuning alone has been shown to move
   quantum kernels from below-classical to competitive. This is the highest
   expected-value experiment available and is cheap: the kernel matrix is one
   matrix product.

2. KERNEL-TARGET ALIGNMENT as the model-selection criterion, instead of
   accuracy or AUC. Aligns the kernel to the labels directly and is standard
   practice in the quantum kernel literature.

3. DESCRIPTOR CHOICE. PCA on correlation vectors is a generic reduction that
   discards network structure. Graph-derived descriptors (modularity,
   participation coefficient, network-level summaries) may carry structure the
   quantum feature map can exploit, where raw principal components do not.

4. PROJECTED QUANTUM KERNELS [6]. Measure reduced density matrices rather than
   full-state overlaps. Specifically designed to resist the exponential
   concentration documented in FINDING 01, and directly applicable here.

5. COHORT BREADTH. ADHD-200 under the same protocol (FINDING for datasets).
   Two disorders, one protocol, is a stronger paper than one disorder with a
   marginally better number.

Note that levers 1, 2 and 4 all attack concentration, which FINDING 01 measured
as the dominant failure mode. That convergence is a good sign they are the
right things to try.
