FINDING 09 — LITERATURE SURVEY AND ARCHITECTURE TAXONOMY
=========================================================
Date: 2026-08-28
Purpose: name the approaches evaluated here, and situate them against what is
         actually published on these cohorts.


WHAT THE LITERATURE ACTUALLY CONTAINS
-------------------------------------
A targeted search for quantum machine learning applied to the ABIDE connectome
returns nothing. There is no published quantum method operating on ABIDE
functional connectivity that we could find, and therefore no positive quantum
result on this cohort to reproduce or exceed.

Adjacent work exists, and it is worth being precise about what it is:

1. "Lightweight quantized CNNs ... on ABIDE-1" (Mathematics 12:2886, 2024).
   QUANTIZED, not quantum — integer weight quantisation for efficiency. This
   is frequently miscited as a quantum result. It is not one.

2. CQ-CNN, hybrid classical-quantum CNN for Alzheimer's from 3-D structural
   MRI, reporting 97.5% with 13.7K parameters. Structural MRI, different
   disorder, no connectome, no site-held-out protocol.

3. Quantum kernel SVM on UCLA-CNP morphometric and EEG features, reporting
   AUC 0.957 for ADHD vs control. Not connectivity, and not ABIDE.

4. Choi et al. (KBS 310:112922, 2025) and Park et al. (arXiv:2509.00711)
   apply quantum circuits to per-region rs-fMRI time series directly, with no
   inter-regional graph.

The strong ABIDE numbers are all classical, and they come from one family of
architectures: POPULATION GRAPHS. Parisot et al. (MICCAI 2017; MedIA 48:117,
2018) place SUBJECTS on the graph rather than brain regions — node features
from imaging, edges from phenotypic and imaging similarity — and report
accuracy around 0.70 under proper evaluation. Reported figures above ~0.85 on
ABIDE almost always use random splits rather than site-held-out splits and are
not comparable to the protocol used here.


TAXONOMY OF APPROACHES IN THIS STUDY
------------------------------------
Three architectures are evaluated. They are named here so that the negative
results remain attributable to the specific design that produced them.

RQT — Region-level Quantum Topology
    nodes    200 brain regions of a single subject
    features per-region temporal PCA of the BOLD signal
    edges    quantum fidelity between regional states
    read-out pooling over regions
    This is the architecture of the original filing and manuscript.
    Result: chance (FINDINGS 02, 05, 06). Root cause is the node features,
    which carry no diagnostic signal (AUC 0.459 across all 200 regions).

SQK — Subject-level Quantum Kernel
    no graph; quantum fidelity between subjects forms an SVM kernel
    Result: no reproducible advantage over classical kernels across four
    cohorts, and the single positive result reverses sign on the largest
    (FINDING 08).

QPG — Quantum Population Graph                                    [new]
    nodes    subjects
    features that subject's full connectivity profile — the representation
             measured to carry signal (AUC 0.693)
    edges    quantum fidelity between subjects, optionally gated by
             phenotypic agreement
    task     transductive node classification over the population
    Motivated directly by the two measured failures: it moves the informative
    representation onto the nodes, and confines the quantum kernel to the
    edges, where the narrow register FINDING 01 requires is natural.


WHY QPG IS THE RIGHT NEXT ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------
It is the only one of the three whose design follows from the measurements
rather than from the original assumption:

- FINDING 06 showed node features were the binding constraint. QPG gives nodes
  the connectivity profile, which is the representation that separates classes.
- FINDING 01 showed fidelity needs a narrow register. QPG uses 8 qubits for
  edges only, so node capacity is not limited by qubit count.
- FINDING 08 showed dimensionality compression dominated every quantum effect.
  QPG node features are 128-dimensional, not 8.

It is also the architecture with the strongest classical track record on these
cohorts, which makes the comparison meaningful: a quantum edge definition is
tested against classical edge definitions inside a design that is known to work.

The comparison includes a NO-GRAPH MLP control on identical features. Without
it, a graph method can appear to succeed when the features alone were doing the
work.
