FINDING 23 — QUANTUM GRAPH TRANSFORMER: TRAINS ON SYNTHETIC, FAILS ON COHORTS
==============================================================================
Status: implemented, debugged, evaluated; EXCLUDED from the paper as a
        comparison arm and reported only as confirmation of FINDING 06
Date:   2026-08-29
Code:   src/qagta/quantum/graph_transformer.py, scripts/run_graph_transformer.py
Raw:    results/qgt/{abide,adhd,ucla,ucla_fixed}/qgt_results.json


WHY IT WAS BUILT
----------------
Every quantum construct evaluated earlier scores a region pair by the overlap
of two separately prepared states. That form has two measured limitations:
concentration as the register widens (FINDING 01) and invariance to anything
applied after the encoding (FINDING 19).

A graph transformer avoids both. Query and key are encoded into ONE register
and interact through entangling gates before measurement, so the logit is
<psi(x_i,x_j)| Z_0 Z_n |psi(x_i,x_j)> rather than |<psi_i|psi_j>|^2. The
trainable layers sit between data-dependent operations on both nodes, so
neither cancellation nor pairwise concentration applies.


FOUR DEFECTS FOUND BY MEASUREMENT, NOT INSPECTION
-------------------------------------------------
Diagnostic: can the model learn synthetic graph tasks it should?

  1. INERT SECOND DATA LAYER. RZ, CZ and the Z observable are all diagonal in
     the computational basis, so a diagonal layer placed immediately before a Z
     measurement is exactly inert. Gradients measured 1e-10. Fixed by using RY
     for the second data layer and adding a basis-change rotation before
     read-out.

  2. KEY BARELY REACHED THE READ-OUT. Measuring Z_0 alone (a query qubit) left
     the key influencing the score only through the entangling ring: logit
     spread across different keys was 0.006. Fixed by measuring the correlator
     Z_0 Z_n, one qubit from each node. Spread rose to 0.114 (19x).

  3. READ-OUT COLLAPSED THE GRAPH. mean(dim=1) reduced 200 nodes to
     node_qubits numbers, dividing any localised signal by the node count.
     Synthetic evidence: a task whose label is the mean over regions trained
     fine (0.827); a task whose label depends on ONE region sat at chance
     (0.493) for BOTH arms. Fixed with a mean/max/std read-out, self-loops, and
     a residual connection.

  4. PERMUTATION SYMMETRY. Even with the above, "region k carries the signal"
     is inexpressible when all regions are interchangeable. Added learned
     per-region embeddings; the localised task went from unlearnable
     (train 0.713) to fitted but not generalising (train 0.850, test 0.527).

After the fixes the architecture demonstrably trains: on the global synthetic
task the quantum arm reaches 0.833 against 0.853 classical.


THE COHORT RESULT
-----------------
  cohort      Q-Attention   C-Attention   majority   verdict
  ---------   -----------   -----------   --------   -------------------
  ABIDE-I         0.488         0.516      0.512     both at chance
  ADHD-200        0.562         0.562      0.561     both F1 = 0.000
  UCLA-CNP        0.451         0.446      0.549     both below majority
  UCLA (fixed)    0.447         0.521      0.549     both below majority

On ADHD-200 both arms return F1 exactly 0.000 with accuracy exactly the
majority rate: single-class prediction on every fold, identically. No paired
test is even computable.


WHY IT IS NOT REPORTED AS A COMPARISON ARM
------------------------------------------
BOTH arms fail, together, on every cohort. A quantum-specific defect cannot
produce that. Where neither arm classifies, the comparison carries no
information about quantum against classical, and reporting it as a tie would
be misleading.

This is the distinction that matters: PLAN B is a genuine null -- both arms
train, neither wins -- and it belongs in the paper. QGT is a failed
experiment, and it does not.


WHAT IT IS WORTH
----------------
It is the strongest confirmation of FINDING 06 in the study. A seventh
architecture, whose attention mechanism provably trains on synthetic graphs,
and which avoids BOTH measured limitations of the fidelity construction, still
sits at chance on these node features. Every graph-based model tested now
agrees: GCN+Pearson 0.494, GCN+RBF 0.508, Quantum+GAT 0.506, Quantum+GCN
0.508, Q-Attention 0.488.

That is what a binding constraint on the INPUT looks like. The node features,
not the graph model, are what limits this pipeline.

Reported in the paper as one subsection under FINDING 06's section, not as a
seventh architectural family.


A NOTE ON COST AND A MISTAKE
-----------------------------
The first cohort runs used k=4 neighbours and 12 epochs, reduced from k=8 and
20 to fit an eight-hour REST-meta-MDD run into three. That reduction probably
made a failing configuration worse: the UCLA smoke test at k=8 had reached
0.522, above chance, while k=4 gave 0.451. The re-test after the fixes used
k=8 and still failed, so the conclusion stands, but the intermediate runs at
k=4 should not be quoted.
