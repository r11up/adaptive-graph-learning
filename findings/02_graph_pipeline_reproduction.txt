WHAT REPRODUCES
---------------
The classical arm reproduces and slightly exceeds the manuscript:
  SVM linear   measured 0.653   manuscript 0.58
  SVM RBF      measured 0.627   manuscript 0.62

This validates the components that could have failed silently: subject
loading, ASD/control label mapping (DX_GROUP 1/2 -> 1/0), site assignment,
and Leave-Site-Out fold construction. The data carries the signal claimed.


WHAT DOES NOT
-------------
All four graph configurations sit at chance (accuracy 0.494-0.508). The
manuscript reports F1 = 0.72 for the proposed model; measured is 0.230.

Per-fold behaviour identifies the failure mode: folds collapse to a single
predicted class. Either all-control (F1 = 0.000, specificity 1.000) or all-ASD
(F1 ~ 0.65, specificity 0.000). The models are not learning a decision
boundary. Note that a degenerate all-positive predictor scores F1 ~ 0.65 on a
balanced set, which is why F1 alone is a misleading headline metric here —
accuracy and specificity expose it immediately.


CAUSES IDENTIFIED
-----------------
1. Fidelity collapse upstream (see FINDING 01). At 16 qubits the topology is
   effectively random, so propagation has no meaningful structure to exploit.

2. Implementation divergence from the patent. The patent specifies an edge
   kernel integrating "cosine similarity, learnable transformations, attention
   scores, AND quantum fidelity" — four terms. The connectome pipeline as first
   written seeded k-NN topology from fidelity ALONE, so fidelity's collapse
   left nothing to order neighbours by. Fixed via --topology mixed.

3. Read-out bottleneck, independent of the above. Global mean pooling averages
   200 regional feature vectors into one graph embedding, discarding which
   region carries what. The SVM comparator sees all 19,900 pairwise
   correlations. A meaningful part of the 0.65-vs-0.23 gap is therefore
   read-out capacity, not topology quality, and would persist even with healthy
   fidelity.


HONEST INTERPRETATION
---------------------
This is not evidence that quantum-derived topology fails. It is evidence that
this configuration destroys the metric it depends on, and that the graph-level
read-out discards most of the available signal. Both are fixable; neither was
fixed by the configuration as specified in the manuscript.

It does mean the manuscript's Table I cannot currently be reproduced from this
implementation, and the F1 = 0.72 figure should not be relied on until the
above are addressed and re-measured.

NOTE ON THE REALISTIC CEILING
-----------------------------
Reference [5] is important context for the manuscript's claims. Under
leave-site-out validation on ABIDE, published accuracy clusters around 0.65-0.70.
The measured SVM here (0.671 accuracy) sits squarely in that range. Reported
ABIDE accuracies above ~0.85 in the literature almost always use random splits
rather than site-held-out splits, and are not comparable. Any claim materially
above ~0.70 under true leave-site-out warrants scrutiny.
