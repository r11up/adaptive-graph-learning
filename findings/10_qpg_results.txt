WHAT THE REDESIGN ACHIEVED
--------------------------
Substantial, and worth stating plainly. On ABIDE, held-out AUC across the
three architectures:

  RQT (region-level quantum topology)      ~0.50   chance
  SQK (subject-level quantum kernel)        0.693
  QPG node features, no graph               0.729   best in this study

Moving the informative representation onto the nodes, as FINDING 06 indicated,
took the pipeline from chance to 0.729 AUC. That is above the ~0.65-0.70 band
that published leave-site-out ABIDE results occupy. The architectural diagnosis
was correct and the fix worked.

The gain came from the node features. It did not come from the quantum kernel,
and it did not come from the graph.


THE QUANTUM EDGES UNDERPERFORM
------------------------------
On both well-powered cohorts the quantum edge definition is significantly worse
than classical edge definitions and worse than no graph at all. On MDD, with 25
folds, it loses all three comparisons at p < 0.005.

ADHD-200 again runs the other way — quantum best on F1 and AUC, 6/7 folds
against the MLP, p = 0.078. This is the same pattern as FINDING 08: a positive
result on the smallest cohort, contradicted by the larger ones. Across the two
studies, every quantum win has appeared on a cohort with 7-10 folds and
reversed on cohorts with 20-25.


THE GRAPH ITSELF DOES NOT HELP
------------------------------
The no-graph MLP is the best model on both large cohorts, beating every
population-graph variant including the classical ones. Under Leave-Site-Out on
these data, propagating information between subjects does not improve on
treating them independently.

This is why the MLP control mattered. Without it the comparison would have been
"quantum edges versus classical edges", and correlation edges at 0.587 would
have looked like a reasonable baseline to beat. The control shows the entire
graph stage is costing performance, which changes what the study concludes.


STATUS OF THE CENTRAL CLAIM
---------------------------
Three architectures, four cohorts, and every lever tested. No configuration
shows a reproducible quantum advantage over matched classical baselines. Two
positive results appeared, both on small cohorts, and both reversed on larger
ones with more folds.

What the work does establish, and what is defensible to publish:

1. A measured design constraint: fidelity's usable range collapses as the
   register widens (FINDING 01), with the operating window quantified.
2. A diagnosis of why region-level quantum topology fails on fMRI, isolated to
   the node features by direct measurement (FINDING 06).
3. A corrected architecture reaching 0.729 AUC under leave-site-out, above the
   published band for this protocol.
4. A negative result on quantum advantage that is well-powered, pre-specified
   in its comparisons, paired-tested, and replicated across four cohorts.
