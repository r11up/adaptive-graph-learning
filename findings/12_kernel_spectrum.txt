FINDING 12 — WHY THE KERNELS TIE: THEY OCCUPY THE SAME FUNCTION CLASS
======================================================================
Status: measured, 400 ABIDE subjects, 8 selected features
Date:   2026-08-28
Figure: fig_kernel_spectrum


THE DIAGNOSTIC
--------------
A Gram matrix's participation ratio, (sum lambda)^2 / sum lambda^2, counts how
many directions the kernel actually uses. Near 1 the kernel is nearly constant
and every subject looks alike (underfitting); near n it approaches the identity
and every subject is its own cluster (memorising). Useful kernels sit between.

RESULT
------
Both kernels traverse the same range, from an effective dimension of ~1 to ~350
out of 400. Both possess a usable window. Quantum fidelity at bandwidth 0.15
reaches effective dimension 10.8; the RBF kernel at gamma = 0.125 reaches 8.2.
At that matched setting their normalised eigenvalue spectra are nearly
indistinguishable.

This explains the matched-feature tie of FINDING 11 mechanistically rather than
statistically. On these data the quantum fidelity kernel is not a qualitatively
different similarity measure. It is a reparameterisation that sweeps the same
family of Gram matrices, with encoding bandwidth playing the role gamma plays
for RBF. Once both are tuned to the same effective dimension, equivalent
performance is the expected outcome, not a surprise.


A CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER READING
----------------------------------
A first pass over three bandwidths (0.05, 0.5, 2.0) appeared to show the
quantum kernel jumping from effective dimension 1.6 straight to 162.6, skipping
the useful range entirely — which would have meant no viable operating point.
Sampling the bandwidth axis more finely shows that is wrong: 0.10 and 0.15 land
at 5.4 and 10.8, squarely inside the window. The apparent gap was an artefact of
a coarse grid.

Recorded because the coarse-grid version was briefly the working explanation,
and it would have supported a stronger and false claim — that the quantum
kernel has no usable regime at all.


PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE
---------------------
Bandwidth is decisive, and an unreported default is likely to be wrong. At
bandwidth 1.0 the quantum kernel sits at effective dimension 289 of 400, close
to the identity, where it memorises the training set. Published quantum-kernel
results that do not state how bandwidth was chosen should be read with that in
mind, since the default is squarely in the memorising regime.
