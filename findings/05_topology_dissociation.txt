Status: measured, full cohort, 4-configuration ablation

Data:   ABIDE I, 1035 subjects, 20 sites, Leave-Site-Out, 30 epochs/fold

RESULT
------
  configuration              MAD      F1      accuracy
  ------------------------   ------   -----   --------
  4 qubits, fidelity-only    0.002    0.197   0.508
  4 qubits, mixed kernel     0.054    0.133   0.512
  8 qubits, mixed kernel     0.043    0.292   0.495
  16 qubits, mixed kernel    0.144    0.230   0.506


THE DISSOCIATION
----------------
Replacing fidelity-only topology seeding with the specified mixed kernel raises
MAD by up to 72x (0.002 -> 0.144). Node representations no longer collapse
toward a common mean; over-smoothing is substantially relieved.

Accuracy does not move. All four configurations sit within 0.02 of chance.

Over-smoothing was therefore a genuine, repairable defect that was NOT the
binding constraint on classification.


WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN EITHER METRIC ALONE
----------------------------------------------
A study measuring only accuracy would conclude quantum-derived topology does
not help. A study measuring only MAD would conclude the fix worked. Measuring
both localises the failure: the topology was repaired, and the bottleneck is
downstream of graph propagation.

Prime suspect: the read-out. Global mean pooling averages 200 regional feature
vectors into one graph embedding, so whichever region carries the
discriminative pattern is averaged away. The correlation SVM on the same folds
keeps all 19,900 pairwise features and reaches 0.671 accuracy. The gap between
0.506 and 0.671 is a capacity gap in the read-out, not a topology-quality gap.

Note also that MAD is highest at 16 qubits (0.144), where FINDING 01 shows
fidelity is fully collapsed. That is consistent rather than contradictory: with
fidelity contributing nothing, the mixed kernel falls back on cosine similarity
of the 16-dimensional latents, which is more informative than the 4-dimensional
case. It is further evidence that MAD is being driven by the cosine term, not
by the quantum contribution.


WHAT TO TEST NEXT
-----------------
1. Replace global mean pooling with a read-out that preserves regional
   identity: concatenated node features, attention pooling, or a fixed
   region-ordered flatten.
2. Report MAD alongside accuracy in any over-smoothing claim. They dissociate,
   so one does not stand in for the other.
